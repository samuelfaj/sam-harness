# Incident response

1. Stabilize the system and stop automated promotion.
2. Record the deployed digest, active configuration, affected users, and first known bad signal.
3. Preserve logs and receipts without collecting unrelated sensitive data.
4. Choose rollback, forward fix, or containment from the current data compatibility state.
5. Verify recovery with technical and business signals.
6. Record the detection gap, failed control, owner, and bounded follow-up.
7. Test the repaired control before closing the incident.
